Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy describes how C50 Academy, operated by Sunil Rajput, handles information through its learning website and Android application. For privacy questions or requests, contact carrierdds50@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and profile: your name, email address, account identifier, authentication credentials and session information, and profile information such as an avatar if supplied. Password authentication is handled through Supabase Auth.
  • Learning activity: batch enrollments, lecture progress, completion status, watch history, and activity used to calculate learning statistics and streaks.
  • Purchases: batch and user identifiers, amount, currency, payment status, payment method, Razorpay order/payment identifiers, verification evidence and webhook event records. Where checkout is offered, Razorpay handles the payment details you enter. C50 does not store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking password.
  • Technical information: hosting, authentication, video and payment providers may process IP addresses, browser/device information, request timestamps and diagnostic or security logs when delivering their services.
  • Support: information you choose to send by email, including your contact details and the content of your request.

2. How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts, deliver courses, save learning progress, verify payments, manage access to purchased batches, respond to support requests, investigate failures or abuse, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. The Play Store app accesses existing course entitlements; payment functionality may be available on the website or direct-install Android distribution.

3. Service providers and sharing

Information is processed by the providers needed to operate these features:

  • Supabase: authentication, account/course records and file storage.
  • Render: hosting the website and backend, including operational request logs.
  • Razorpay: checkout, payment processing, verification and fraud prevention where purchases are supported.
  • YouTube/Google: embedded lecture playback, which may involve cookies, device/network information and viewing interactions under Google's policy.

External image hosts receive network requests when their images are displayed. Opening social links or other external pages sends you to services governed by their own policies. Authorized administrators access records necessary to operate the academy. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to investigate fraud or protect users and the service. The current application does not implement a sale-of-personal-data feature or an advertising tracking SDK.

4. Local storage and device access

The website uses browser storage and the Android app uses local app storage to maintain sessions and, where applicable, recover pending purchases. Signing out ends the app's signed-in session; clearing app/browser storage removes local data but does not delete your server account. Embedded providers may use their own cookies or storage.

The learning features do not require access to your contacts, precise location, SMS, call history, camera or microphone. Opening or downloading notes may use your browser or another app on your device.

5. Security and processing locations

We use HTTPS for hosted services, authenticated APIs, database access controls and server-side payment verification. No system can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, according to their infrastructure and privacy terms.

6. Retention and deletion requests

Account and learning records are retained while needed to provide your account and courses. Payment and security records may be retained longer where necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. Backups and provider logs may remain until their retention cycles expire. We do not currently publish a fixed retention period for each record category.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated information, email carrierdds50@gmail.com from your registered email address, with the subject “C50 Academy account deletion request” for deletion. Include only the account details needed to identify you; never send your password, OTP or payment credentials. We may verify ownership before acting. Deletion removes access to the account and its learning history; any records that must be retained and the reason will be explained in our response. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account.

7. Children

C50 Academy is designed for competitive-examination aspirants, not as a service directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

8. Changes and contact

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or email where appropriate.
