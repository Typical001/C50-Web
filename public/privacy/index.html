1. What information do we collect?

We handle information you provide when registering, using courses, making a purchase where available, or contacting us:

  • Account and profile: name, email address, account identifier, authentication credentials and session information, and an avatar if supplied. Password authentication is handled through Supabase Auth.
  • Learning activity: enrollments, lecture progress and completion, watch history, and activity used for learning statistics and streaks.
  • Purchases: user and batch identifiers, amount, currency, payment status and method, Razorpay order/payment identifiers, verification evidence and webhook event records. Razorpay handles the payment credentials entered in Checkout. C50 does not store full card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or banking passwords. See Razorpay’s privacy policy.
  • Technical information: hosting, authentication, video and payment providers may process IP addresses, browser/device information, request timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs to deliver their services.
  • Support: contact details and information you choose to include in your messages. Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment credentials.

We also receive service-related information from providers, such as authentication results and payment confirmations. Our learning features do not ask you to provide sensitive personal details such as religious beliefs or health information.

2. How do we use your information?

We use information to create and secure accounts, deliver courses and notes, save progress, verify payments, manage course access, respond to support and privacy requests, troubleshoot failures, prevent fraud or abuse, and meet applicable legal and record-keeping obligations. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it as described below.

Paid checkout is offered on the website and supported direct-install Android builds. The Play Store app accesses existing entitlements and does not offer paid checkout.

3. When and with whom do we share information?

We use service providers to operate the platform:

  • Supabase: authentication, database records and file storage.
  • Render: website/backend hosting and operational request logs.
  • Razorpay: payment processing, verification and fraud prevention where checkout is offered.
  • YouTube/Google: embedded lecture playback and Google Drive notes, which may involve network/device information, cookies and interactions under Google’s policies.

External image hosts receive requests when images load. Opening external notes or other links takes you to services governed by their own policies. Authorized administrators access records needed to operate the institute. We may disclose information when required by law, to investigate fraud or protect users, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable obligations. The current application does not implement advertising tracking SDKs or a sale-of-personal-data feature.

4. Cookies, local storage and device access

The website uses browser storage and Android uses local app storage for sessions, preferences and, where applicable, pending purchase recovery. Signing out clears the signed-in interface; clearing local storage does not delete your server account. Embedded video and payment providers may use their own cookies or storage.

Learning features do not require contacts, precise location, SMS, call history, camera or microphone access. Opening or downloading notes may use your browser or another app.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account and learning information while needed to provide the service. After ownership is verified for an account-deletion request, we delete the account and associated learning data within 30 days.

Our standard retention period for C50-held payment records associated with a deletion request is 30 days from verification of account ownership. After that period, records are deleted or irreversibly anonymized unless a legal obligation or unresolved dispute requires retention. Support explains any exception, the records affected and the applicable period. This is not a promise to delete every purchase record 30 days after payment.

Records held independently by providers, including provider-managed backups and logs, follow their own retention requirements. See account-deletion steps and retention details.

6. How do we protect information?

We use HTTPS for hosted services, authenticated APIs, database access controls and server-side payment verification. These safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials private and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access. Providers may process information outside your country according to their infrastructure and privacy terms.

7. Adults-only eligibility

Our services are intended for people aged 18 or older. People under 18 should not register or use the services. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children or market the service to them. If you believe an under-18 user has supplied information, contact carrierdds50@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate steps to restrict the account and delete the information, subject to applicable retention obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out or processing permitted on another applicable basis. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or unresolved disputes.

You can update supported profile details in the service or contact us for help. Requests may require identity verification; never send your password or payment credentials.

9. Browser and external-service choices

You can manage cookies and stored website data through your browser settings. Blocking or clearing storage may sign you out or affect features. Embedded providers offer their own privacy controls. Opening a Google Drive note may require permission from its owner; publicly shared links can be redistributed, and enrollment checks do not make those links private.

10. Updates to this notice

We may update this notice when our services or practices change. The updated date appears at the top. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or email where appropriate.

12. Review, correct or delete your information

Email carrierdds50@gmail.com to request access or correction. For deletion, follow our account-deletion instructions and use the subject “C50 Academy account deletion request”, preferably from your registered email. Include only the information needed to identify your account. If you cannot access that email, explain this so support can help verify ownership.

Opening the deletion page does not submit a request: you must send the email. You do not need to sign in or reinstall the app to request deletion. Uninstalling does not delete your account. Deletion removes account access and learning history; any retained records and reasons are explained in our response.
