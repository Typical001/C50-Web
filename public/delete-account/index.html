Request account deletion

C50 Academy (Carrier-50) is operated by Sunil Rajput. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data without signing in or reinstalling the app.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

How to send a request

  1. Email carrierdds50@gmail.com, preferably from the email address registered to your C50 account.
  2. Use the subject C50 Academy account deletion request. State that you want your account and associated data deleted and include your registered email address.
  3. Our support team verifies account ownership before deleting information. If you cannot access your registered email, explain this in your request so support can help verify ownership. Never send passwords, OTPs, recovery links, card details or payment PINs.
  4. After ownership is verified, we complete account and associated-data deletion within 30 days and notify you by email.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, copy carrierdds50@gmail.com into your email service and use the subject above. Opening this page or the email draft does not submit a request; you must send the email.

What will be deleted

  • Your login account, profile details and profile image stored by C50.
  • Your batch enrollments, lecture progress, completion records, watch history and learning streaks.
  • Other account-linked personal content controlled by C50, subject to the limited retention exceptions below.

Deletion removes access to enrolled courses, including paid batches, and your saved learning history. It is not temporary deactivation. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. A deletion request is not automatically a refund request; contact support about any payment dispute.

Payment records and other retained data

Our standard retention period for C50-held payment records associated with a deletion request is 30 days from verification of account ownership. These include transaction identifiers, amount, currency and payment status. After that period, these records are deleted or irreversibly anonymized unless a legal obligation or an unresolved dispute requires retention.

If an exception applies, support will explain which records are retained, why, and the applicable retention period. Records held independently by payment or hosting providers, including provider-managed backups and logs, follow their own retention requirements and are not subject to C50's 30-day promise. See our Privacy Policy for service providers.

Questions or follow-up

Reply to your original request or email carrierdds50@gmail.com. Keep your request acknowledgement for reference.
