# Account deletion request page

Public target: https://c50-web.onrender.com/delete-account

This is a manual email request flow, not an automatic deletion API or background job. Requests go to carrierdds50@gmail.com. The owner confirmed 30 days for processing and payment-record retention on November 1, 2026; the page defines the period as starting when ownership is verified. Confirm the policy is compatible with actual legal/accounting obligations before publishing. Legal/dispute exceptions and independent provider retention are disclosed, not given invented fixed periods.

## Manual fulfilment checklist

1. Record receipt and verify account ownership without requesting passwords, OTPs or recovery tokens. Offer an alternative verification process when registered-email access is lost.
2. Confirm the exact account and linked data, explain loss of course access, and note the verified date and 30-day deadline. Avoid collecting extra identity data unnecessarily.
3. Determine any legally required payment/security/dispute retention with the owner/accountant. Inform the requester of the data, reason and applicable period. Do not delete records subject to a valid hold.
4. Use a reviewed administrative deletion procedure to revoke sessions, remove the login/profile and associated learning records and owned files, and delete or irreversibly anonymize eligible payment data. Review foreign keys and shared files first. Revocation must account for C50's custom Express sessions as well as provider sessions. No deletion procedure was added or executed by this page-only task.
5. Address provider-managed records/backups and keep any necessary minimal request audit with an approved retention period. Confirm completion and any retained exceptions by email.

Do not silently close a request, merely deactivate an account, or claim data was erased without verification. Automated fulfilment and the operational deletion procedure remain separate work.
